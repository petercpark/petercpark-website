## Mindless Rubik's Cube Solution

I started making this book in fifth grade at the suggestion of my dad. He said I was spending too much time cubing and gave me the idea to start a book about how to solve a Rubik's cube. The vision for my book was to make a completely visual Rubik's cube guide that allowed anyone to solve a cube by following the pictures, just like a lego manual. I used a really old version of Photoshop to draw the cubes, and Microsoft PowerPoint to make the book. I worked on this book on and off for approximately three years, and self-published the book on Amazon right before starting eighth grade, on June 24, 2018. I thus became a "published author" at the age of 12. I do not recommend that anyone buy this book for the purpose of learning how to solve the Rubik's cube because a free video tutorial on YouTube does a much better job. However, I am still proud of this book and see it as a work of art. This book can be thought of as a novelty item and a milestone achievement of my life. Here is a link: https://www.amazon.com/Mindless-Rubiks-Solution-Peter-Changhee/dp/1719487650

## U.S. Design Patent USD989072S1

The design patent protects the "ornamental design" or the layout of the keys and shape of the board of the Uni by StenoKeyboards. I personally drew the patent drawings. I learned a lot about how to draw design patent drawings. I learned that solid lines in a drawing means it is protected and dashed or dotted lines means it is not protected. Others cannot copy the solid lines, but can copy the dotted lines. Here is a link: https://patents.google.com/patent/USD989072S1
