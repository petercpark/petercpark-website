# Career

## StenoKeyboards Founder

I made a stenography keyboard and started selling them in February 2021. This was the birth of the company, [StenoKeyboards](https://www.stenokeyboards.com/). I continue to own and operate the company.

## Tulare County District Attorney's Office

In the beginning of 2023, I applied to the Tulare County District Attorney's Office for their Post Bar Law Clerk position. In August of 2023, after taking the bar exam, I started working as a law clerk. I received my bar results in November of 2023 and was sworn in as a Deputy District Attorney in December of 2023. I continued working here until December 2025.

## Los Angeles County District Attorney's Office

I started working for the LADA's office as a Deputy District Attorney in February 2026.
